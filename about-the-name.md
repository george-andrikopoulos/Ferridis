# About the name

Ferridis is named for **Alexandros Ferridis**, the high school teacher in Greece who shaped how I think about my work and the way I treat the people I do it with. He passed away from cancer on the 8th of November 2012.

He was the kind of teacher whose influence you only fully understand much later. He didn't just teach a subject — he taught a way of approaching problems carefully, and of treating other people's intelligence with respect. The instinct to share what you build rather than hoard it, to work in the open rather than behind closed doors — that came from him.

This protocol is built on a belief that good infrastructure should be generous: open, well-attributed, useful to anyone who needs it. That belief is his. Naming it Ferridis is the only way I know to keep his name moving through a world he isn't in anymore.

— George Andrikopoulos
